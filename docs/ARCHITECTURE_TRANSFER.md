# Architectural research transfer boundary

MORSE is an independent research line. Prior orchestration research may inform its methodology and generate hypotheses, but prior results are not treated as MORSE evidence.

## Reusable methods

The following practices are intentionally adopted as methodological primitives:

- deterministic seeded execution;
- explicit topology invariants;
- instrumentation and evidence schemas;
- fault injection;
- provenance and artifact hashing;
- analysis freeze before comparative efficacy testing;
- separation of implementation, verification, and empirical claims;
- independent verification where feasible;
- explicit handling of stale or mismatched artifact identities.

## Boundary

`Prior research -> MORSE hypothesis/design input` is permitted.

`Prior research result -> MORSE efficacy conclusion` is not permitted without an independent MORSE test.

MORSE results likewise must not be silently incorporated into another project's claims.
