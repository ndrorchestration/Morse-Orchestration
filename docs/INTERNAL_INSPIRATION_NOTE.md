# Internal Inspiration Note

This note is intentionally outside the scientific rationale.

The MOLI/cloverleaf intuition was partly prompted by thinking about Nikola Tesla's alternating-current and polyphase systems: independently varying processes can produce useful system-level behavior through controlled phase relationships.

This is an analogy for design intuition only. Tesla's electrical engineering does not constitute evidence for MORSE, and no historical or physical equivalence is claimed.

For scientific documentation, use the operational terms loop, cadence, phase, gate, hub, coupling, and measured interaction rather than relying on the analogy.
